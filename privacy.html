3. What we collect

To see and receive your calculator numbers we need your email (and usually a first name). Results are calculated in your browser from hourly rate and weekly hours. We use the email so we can send and save those numbers.

The form collects:

  • first name
  • email address (required to get your numbers)
  • business type (optional)
  • the hourly rate and weekly hours you entered, plus the calculated figures
  • whether you ticked the marketing box

We do not collect special-category data (including health or birth-related information) on these pages. Business type is optional free text. Do not put health or similar details in that field.

If you buy the pack, we receive the details needed to complete the purchase and deliver it (typically name, email and billing details). Card details are entered on Stripe and go to Stripe, not to Attenger Ltd.

4. Why we use it, and the legal basis

  • Sending and saving your calculator numbers — we ask for your email so we can do that (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b), or legitimate interests in responding to a request you initiated). You will not see results without an email.
  • Marketing emails about plug-and-play AI workflows — only if you tick the box. The box is left unticked. Legal basis: consent (Article 6(1)(a) and PECR). Emails come from Attenger Ltd / Emma Fell, Ethical AI for Business.
  • Taking payment and delivering the pack — performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Tax and accounting records of a purchase — legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)).

5. How long we keep it

  • Calculator leads (no purchase): 12 months, then we delete them.
  • Buyers: as long as needed for the purchase and for UK tax, typically 6 years.

6. Who we share it with

We use two named processors on these pages:

  • Formspree (United States) — receives the calculator form when you ask for your numbers. Form: formspree.io/f/mjybzjyl. Formspree privacy: formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.
  • Stripe (United States; card payments on the sales page) — processes card payments. Card data goes to Stripe, not Attenger. Stripe privacy: stripe.com/gb/privacy.

We do not sell your personal data.

7. Transfers outside the UK

Formspree and Stripe are US companies, so using the calculator or paying by card involves a transfer of personal data to the United States.

We rely on a written processor contract (a data processing agreement, or DPA) with each of them and, where they participate, the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Stripe publishes that it participates in the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. We have not verified Formspree on the public Data Privacy Framework list. If Formspree is not on that list (including the UK Extension), a UK International Data Transfer Addendum (IDTA) to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses is required for that transfer and must be in place before we send personal data to Formspree.

8. If you buy the Setup Pack (the “postcard” rule)

The pack is how-tos and workflow designs that you run in your own accounts. You remain the controller of any personal data you put into those tools (for example, enquiries or reviews).

Make, Google, and Anthropic (used via Make) are the buyer’s processors, not ours. We do not send emails or other messages to your customers automatically. Enquiry replies are drafts only; you still send them. Think of a postcard: we supply the template, you post it.
